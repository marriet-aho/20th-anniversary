Subject: InfoPortal: replace the Anniversary Portal app and allow visitors to post (5 minutes)

Hi,

Please do these two things on the InfoPortal site.

1. REPLACE THE APP
   App Catalog > Apps for SharePoint > upload the attached spfx-anniversary-portal.sppkg (version 1.0.10.0) > choose Replace > Deploy.
   It is the same app with fixes. It needs no new permissions.

2. LET VISITORS POST AND REACT
   Do this on two lists only: BoardMessages and GalleryReactions.
   For each list:
   a. Gear icon > List settings > Permissions for this list > Stop Inheriting Permissions.
   b. Tick the Visitors and Members groups > Edit User Permissions > choose Contribute (instead of Read) > OK.
   c. Back in List settings > Advanced settings:
      - Read access: Read all items
      - Create and Edit access: Create items and edit items that were created by the user
      Click OK.

Nothing else on the site changes. Thank you!
